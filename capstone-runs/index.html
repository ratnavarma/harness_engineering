Born in the Clouds of Kalasa

Nestled in the lush green Western Ghats of Karnataka, Kalasa is more than just a temple town—it's the heart of India's coffee country. At Malnad Coffee Store, we bring you the finest hand-picked beans, cultivated under the canopy of ancient trees and nurtured by the monsoon rains.

Our coffee is a tribute to the rich heritage of Chikmagalur, offering a cup that is as deep and complex as the forests it comes from.

Our Signature Roasts

Pure Arabica

Smooth, aromatic, and slightly sweet with notes of caramel and chocolate. Perfect for a relaxing morning.

Malnad Strong Blend

A bold mix of Arabica and Robusta. Earthy, full-bodied, and packs the perfect punch to wake you up.

Traditional Filter Coffee

The authentic South Indian experience. 80% Coffee, 20% Chicory for that thick, rich decoction.
