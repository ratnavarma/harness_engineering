Our Story: From the Hills to the Coast

Driftwood Roasters was born out of a profound love for two things: the unparalleled complexity of Indian specialty coffee and the laid-back, soulful rhythm of life in Goa. Founded by a group of passionate coffee professionals who traded the bustling city life for the serene beaches of the Konkan coast, our roastery is a sanctuary for those who appreciate the finer nuances of a perfectly brewed cup.

The name "Driftwood" reflects our philosophy. Just as driftwood is shaped and refined by the relentless yet gentle forces of the ocean, our coffee is meticulously crafted through a process that respects the natural characteristics of the bean while bringing out its most extraordinary flavors. We believe that great coffee is not just a beverage; it is an experience, a moment of pause in a chaotic world, a connection to the earth and the hands that nurtured it.

Our roastery, located in a restored Portuguese villa in North Goa, is more than just a production facility. It is a community hub, a place where locals and travelers alike gather to share stories, ideas, and, of course, exceptional coffee. The salty sea air, the warm tropical sun, and the vibrant local culture all seep into the very essence of what we do, making every cup of Driftwood coffee a true taste of Goa.

We spend months traveling across the Western Ghats, visiting remote estates in Chikmagalur, Coorg, and the Nilgiris. We build direct, meaningful relationships with farmers who share our dedication to sustainable and ethical agricultural practices. By cutting out the middlemen, we ensure that the farmers receive a fair price for their hard work, and we get access to the most exclusive, high-quality micro-lots available.

Sourcing & Sustainability

At Driftwood Roasters, we recognize that the journey of a coffee bean from a distant hillside to your morning cup is long and complex. We take our responsibility to the environment and the coffee-growing communities very seriously. Our sourcing model is built on the pillars of transparency, equity, and ecological stewardship.

We exclusively purchase shade-grown, bird-friendly coffees. The canopy of native trees not only provides crucial habitat for local wildlife but also slows the maturation of the coffee cherries, allowing them to develop denser, more complex flavor profiles. We prioritize estates that employ organic farming methods, minimizing the use of synthetic fertilizers and pesticides to protect the delicate biodiversity of the Western Ghats.

Our commitment to sustainability extends beyond the farm. In our Goan roastery, we utilize energy-efficient roasting equipment and have implemented a comprehensive zero-waste program. Our packaging is 100% compostable, made from plant-based materials that break down naturally without leaving harmful microplastics behind. We also repurpose our coffee chaff and spent grounds, donating them to local organic farms to be used as nutrient-rich compost.

When you choose Driftwood Roasters, you are not just buying a bag of coffee. You are supporting a supply chain that values human dignity and environmental health. You are helping to preserve the pristine landscapes of India's coffee-growing regions and contributing to the prosperity of the farmers who dedicate their lives to this incredible craft.

Our Roasting Philosophy

Roasting coffee is a delicate dance between art and science. At Driftwood Roasters, we approach this craft with immense respect for the raw material. Our goal is never to impose a "roaster's signature" on the beans, but rather to unlock and highlight the inherent flavors that the farmer worked so hard to cultivate.

We roast in small batches using a state-of-the-art drum roaster, meticulously tracking temperature curves, development time, and rate of rise. Every single origin we offer undergoes rigorous profile testing. We cup dozens of sample roasts, making microscopic adjustments to the heat application and airflow until we find the absolute sweet spot—the point where sweetness, acidity, and body are in perfect harmony.

We believe that freshness is paramount. Coffee is a perishable agricultural product, and its volatile aromatic compounds begin to dissipate shortly after roasting. That is why we operate on a strict roast-to-order model. When you place an order with Driftwood Roasters, your coffee is roasted specifically for you, packaged immediately in bags with one-way degassing valves, and shipped out the very next day. This ensures that you experience the coffee at its absolute peak flavor potential.

Our roasting team is constantly learning, experimenting, and pushing the boundaries of what Indian specialty coffee can be. We host regular public cupping sessions at our Goa roastery, inviting our community to taste alongside us, learn about the roasting process, and develop their own palates. We believe that education and transparency are the keys to elevating the coffee culture in India.

Brew Guides

Great coffee deserves to be brewed with care. We have put together these comprehensive guides to help you get the best possible results at home, no matter which brewing method you prefer. Remember, these are starting points—feel free to experiment with grind size, water temperature, and ratios to find the perfect cup for your taste.

Hario V60 Pour-Over

The V60 is our favorite method for highlighting the delicate floral and fruity notes of our lighter roasts. It produces a clean, bright, and highly articulate cup.

  • Dose: 15g coffee
  • Water: 250g at 93°C (200°F)
  • Grind: Medium-fine (like table salt)
  • Time: 2:30 - 3:00 minutes

Instructions: Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the water. Add the ground coffee and level the bed. Start your timer and pour 45g of water to bloom the coffee. Wait 45 seconds. Slowly pour the remaining water in concentric circles, keeping the water level consistent. Allow the water to draw down completely.

AeroPress

The AeroPress is incredibly versatile and forgiving. This recipe uses the inverted method to produce a rich, full-bodied cup that is perfect for our medium and darker roasts.

  • Dose: 18g coffee
  • Water: 200g at 90°C (194°F)
  • Grind: Medium-fine
  • Time: 2:00 minutes

Instructions: Invert the AeroPress and add the coffee. Pour 50g of water and stir vigorously to ensure all grounds are wet. Wait 30 seconds. Pour the remaining 150g of water and stir gently. Attach the cap with a rinsed paper filter. At 1:30, flip the AeroPress onto your mug and press down slowly for 30 seconds.

French Press

The classic immersion brewer. The French Press allows all the coffee oils to end up in your cup, resulting in a heavy, textured body. Ideal for our Monsoon Malabar and Goan Sunset Blend.

  • Dose: 30g coffee
  • Water: 500g at 95°C (203°F)
  • Grind: Coarse (like sea salt)
  • Time: 4:00 minutes

Instructions: Add the coarse coffee to the carafe. Pour all 500g of water vigorously to ensure all grounds are saturated. Start your timer. At 4:00 minutes, use a spoon to gently break the crust that has formed at the top and scoop away the foam. Place the plunger on top and press down slowly. Pour immediately to stop the extraction.

Espresso

Espresso is an intense, concentrated beverage that requires precision. This is a standard starting recipe for a double shot, but you will need to dial in based on your specific machine and grinder.

  • Dose: 18g coffee
  • Yield: 36g to 40g liquid espresso
  • Grind: Very fine
  • Time: 25 - 30 seconds

Instructions: Grind the coffee into your portafilter. Distribute the grounds evenly and tamp with firm, level pressure. Lock the portafilter into the group head and start the extraction immediately. Look for a slow, syrupy flow that gradually lightens in color. Stop the shot when you reach your target weight.
